SwapLift Support

SwapLift is a high-intensity strength training app that builds a simple weekly program and suggests joint-friendly exercise swaps.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback? Email swapliftapp@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version if you are reporting a problem.

Common questions

Where is my data? Everything you enter is stored only on your device. There is no account and no server. If you delete the app, your data is deleted too, so use Settings > Backup to export a file if you want a copy.

How do I restore Pro on a new device? Open the SwapLift Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases, using the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

How do I cancel my subscription? Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose SwapLift. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

Is this medical advice? No. SwapLift is for general fitness information only. Talk to a physician before starting any exercise program, especially if you have an injury.
