Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SwapLift mobile app ("SwapLift", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. The short version: your training data stays on your device, and we do not collect, store, or sell it.

Information you enter

To build your program, SwapLift lets you enter information such as your training preferences (days per week, split, equipment), a joint or injury area you want to protect (for example knee, lower back, shoulder, elbow, hip, or wrist), your unit preference, and your workout logs (exercises, weights, reps, and dates).

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. It is not sent to us or to any server we operate. SwapLift does not require or offer an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or location.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. SwapLift does not include analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting tools, and it does not make network requests to send your data anywhere. We do not sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Backup files

SwapLift can export your data as a backup file (JSON) and restore from one. When you export, the app hands the file to your device's share sheet, and you choose where it goes (for example Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop, or email). That file, and wherever you choose to send or store it, is under your control and is governed by the privacy practices of the service you choose. We do not receive a copy.

Purchases and subscriptions

SwapLift Pro subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

To manage subscriptions, we may use RevenueCat, Inc. as a payment and subscription processor. If so, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated by the app and your App Store purchase receipts and transaction details, so that it can confirm and restore your subscription status. This information is not linked to your name or email by us. RevenueCat's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

You can erase all SwapLift data at any time with Settings > Reset all data, or by deleting the app. Because we do not hold a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Any backup files you exported must be deleted by you from wherever you saved them.

Children

SwapLift is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security features, such as your passcode and device encryption. Keep your device and any exported backup files secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change significantly affects how data is handled, we will also note it in the app or its release notes.
